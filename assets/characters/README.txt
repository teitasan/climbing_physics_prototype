Quaternius Universal Base Characters — Standard (CC0)

This folder contains the free Standard pack subset used by the prototype:

- Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf (imported with Humanoid BoneMap)
- Hair_SimpleParted.gltf
- Eyebrows_Regular.gltf (available, body already includes eyebrows)

Regular Male / Female are not in the Standard zip (Source pack only).

Locomotion clips come from `assets/animations/quaternius/AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.glb`.
